# Coach notes (teaching points to keep in the app)

Short rules of thumb from the coach that the app should keep repeating. When one of these changes, update the matching text in `src/data/` and this list.

## Man coverage
1. **Know your man-beater routes.** Against man it is always about the routes you like against man: crossers, mesh, bunch cross.
2. **The QB has to understand the matchup.** Man means one-on-one. The QB reads each matchup and throws to the best one.

Where these show up in the app:
- Concept card **Beating Man** (`src/data/concepts.json`, id `man`).
- The **Man tell** text shown after a Read the Defense answer and in the callout under the field (`src/data/copy.json`, `tell.man.text`).

## Learning order
Start with the basics, then build up:
1. **Route numbers**: see a route, pick its number (and the reverse). This is the route tree, numbers 0 to 9.
2. **Formations**: see a formation, pick its name (and the reverse).
3. Then the call quiz, reading the defense, beating the coverage, and the concept cards.
